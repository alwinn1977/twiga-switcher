The terminology and scores in this package were authored for TwigaSwitcher
and are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0. No third-party word list
was copied to create this package.

Version 1.1.0 expands developer tools, services, protocols, file formats,
workflow vocabulary, and Russian IT slang. Common Russian inflections are
explicit dictionary entries. Private project identifiers, personal names,
and misspellings from local rules are excluded.

Short ambiguous terms have reduced scores to preserve correctly typed common
words and abbreviations, including мл, МГУ, СВТ, КС, ЕД, гм, and LTD.
