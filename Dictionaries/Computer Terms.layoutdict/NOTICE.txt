Version 1.1.0 expands developer tools, services, protocols, file formats,
workflow vocabulary, and Russian IT slang. Russian inflections are explicit
entries; they are not spelling correction rules. Private project identifiers,
personal names, and misspellings from local rules are not included.

Scoring rubric:
  5200  ubiquitous platform or development term
  4800  broadly used professional term
  4400  common specialized term
  4000  recognizable product, protocol, or punctuation-bearing term
  3600  narrower specialist term

Collision-sensitive short terms can use 2400, 3000, 3400, or 3600 instead
of the category score. These keep common correctly typed words/abbreviations
(such as мл, МГУ, СВТ, КС, ЕД, гм, and LTD) unchanged in either direction.
Scores below 4000 do not initiate early switching while typing.
